About

Hi! I am Kavin.

The first drafts of all the articles, poems, or mini writings I post here are handwritten. These are essentially truth-seeking exercises that I try to do regularly (sort of), through prayer, listening to God, and writing. If it ain’t handwritten, it ain’t here!

If there is one thing I want you to take away, it is this: Write. If you think that your mind is blank and have no clue what to write, perhaps that's your first sentence, "It is hard formulating thoughts, and I do not know what to write". There will be flow after that.

Writing helps organize the thoughts in your mind, and it can take you on a truth-seeking journey you may not have experienced before.

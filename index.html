Discernment

The first drafts of all the articles, poems, or mini writings I post here are handwritten. These are essentially truth-seeking exercises that I try to do regularly (sort of), through prayer, listening to God, and writing.

If there is one thing I want you to take away from this website, it is this: Write.

Writing helps organize the thoughts in your mind, and it can take you on a truth-seeking journey you may not have experienced before.
